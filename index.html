NFL OFFSEASON 2026

Owners Shake Up Schedule: International Game Rules Overhauled

Published May 2026

In a major shift at the Spring League Meetings, NFL team owners voted to eliminate the rule allowing teams to protect home games from moving overseas. Starting soon, every single matchup is fair game for international scheduling as the league pushes toward a target of 10 international games per year by 2027.

Commissioner Roger Goodell hinted that the ultimate blueprint could expand even further, establishing a massive global footprint for pro football.

OTAs Heat Up: Veterans and Rookies Take the Field

Published May 2026

Organized Team Activities (OTAs) are officially in full swing. While these spring practices are strictly non-contact and voluntary, they mark the first critical look at newly drafted rookies mixing into depth charts alongside established veterans. All eyes remain on high-profile quarterbacks adjusting to new systems before mandatory minicamps arrive in June.

  • June Mandatory Minicamps
